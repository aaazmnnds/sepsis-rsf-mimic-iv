RSF-predicted risk scores were used to stratify patients into high-risk (n=43) and low-risk (n=43) groups via median split. The high-risk group showed significantly lower survival probability compared to the low-risk group. At 30 days, survival probability was 79.9% (95% CI: 62.2--100.0) for the high-risk group versus 88.9% (95% CI: 70.6--100.0) for the low-risk group. At 60 days, survival was 79.9% (95% CI: 62.2--100.0) versus 88.9% (95% CI: 70.6--100.0), respectively. The hazard ratio was 1.67 (95% CI: 0.17--16.10, p = 0.660), indicating that high-risk patients had a 67% higher mortality hazard compared to low-risk patients.
