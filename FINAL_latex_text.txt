RSF-predicted risk scores were used to stratify patients into high-risk (n=426) and low-risk (n=426) groups via median split. The high-risk group showed significantly lower survival probability compared to the low-risk group. At 30 days, survival probability was 94.2% (95% CI: 85.7--100.0) for the high-risk group versus 80.8% (95% CI: 75.5--86.4) for the low-risk group. At 60 days, survival was 94.2% (95% CI: 85.7--100.0) versus 68.5% (95% CI: 59.2--79.2), respectively. The hazard ratio was 0.13 (95% CI: 0.03--0.56, p < 0.01).
